## Change scope

Implemented Cranelift-aligned amd64 immediate ALU emission for `AddImm` / `SubImm`:

- Added direct x86 encodings for:
  - `add r64/r32, imm8|imm32`
  - `sub r64/r32, imm8|imm32`
- Updated `vcode/emit/instruction_amd64.mbt` to prefer direct reg-imm forms and only
  fall back to scratch-register materialization for out-of-range immediates.
