## Description

The production allocator has the outer shape of a bundle-based backtracking
allocator, but its core still relies on sorted arrays, per-segment register
probes, shallow splitting, global edge scans, and post-hoc heuristics. Large
functions consequently spend most compilation time in register allocation.

Replace those mechanisms in dependency order. Each child issue is an
independently verified and committed step.

## Design

First remove heuristics that compensate for missing allocator semantics. Then
introduce a range index and merged bundle probe, derive edge transfers from
live edge facts, preserve split lineage with spillsets and real range splits,
and finally replace the misleading `SinglePass` strategy with an independent
linear-time allocator.

Do not preserve compatibility for internal implementation details. Preserve
the public allocation contract and correctness on both production targets.
