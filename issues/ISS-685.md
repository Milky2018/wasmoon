## Description
After empty pre-mark discovery is removed, sweep still visits every live-list
candidate to retire dead identities individually. Allocation-heavy controls have
millions of candidates but very few survivors. Existing identity pages already
hold exact live populations; fully dead pages can be retired as a group.

## Design
Maintain a conservative per-page mark hint alongside existing payload marks.
Use ascending live identities and exact page populations to skip wholly unmarked
pages, retaining the existing dead-header writes. Marked pages retain individual
header checks and retirement. Preserve
stable identities, quota accounting, ordered survivors, free-list bookkeeping,
manual marks, precise/conservative roots and rollback. Invalidate lifetime hints
before freeing pages. Do not change collector thresholds or tracing policy.
Retain only after uninstrumented frozen-parent controls; otherwise remove it.
