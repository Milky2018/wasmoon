## Description
Replace the textual-opcode target skeletons with real MilkIR-to-MachV lowering by migrating the instruction-selection, ABI, and target-policy code currently under `modules/wasmoon/vcode/lower`, `vcode/isa`, and `vcode/abi`.

## Design
Keep target-specific lowering in `x64_target` and `aarch64_target`. Shared helpers may be extracted only after duplication is real and stable; do not introduce `backend`, `isa_backend`, or `backend_common` prematurely. Target modules own ABI policy, register placement, target constraints, legalization, call lowering, and instruction selection.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] `x64_target` lowers the supported MilkIR subset into real MachV instructions and constraints without textual placeholder opcodes.
- [ ] `aarch64_target` lowers the supported MilkIR subset into real MachV instructions and constraints without textual placeholder opcodes.
- [ ] ABI/calling-convention tests from old `vcode/abi`, `vcode/isa`, and `vcode/lower` are ported or tracked.
- [ ] Target modules do not depend on Wasmoon runtime/JIT packages.
- [ ] JIT/interpreter correctness gates remain green for migrated lowering.
