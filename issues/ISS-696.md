## Description
After the independent vector repair, a long i32 data-initialization sample has
1174/1542 main-thread samples in gc_heap_array_set_wide and the remainder in its
segment loop. A single cold probe scales from242us for one initialization to
1.404s for8192; this establishes the stimulus, not an optimization result.

## Design
Share one numeric-data initializer between array.new_data and array.init_data.
Only matching immutable numeric canonical layouts on little-endian hosts may
copy a contiguous checked range. Unknown/legacy layouts retain explicit decoding
and existing setters, barriers and verification. Preserve every caller check,
zero-length/drop/null ordering and single allocation attempt. Do not duplicate
the allocator or change collection policy to improve this benchmark.
