## Description
`EGraph.uses` records use-def edges "for worklist scheduling"
(`modules/milkir/egraph/egraph.mbt:524`), and `rebuild` reconstructs the
whole map from scratch (`egraph.mbt:932`). The map has **zero readers** —
the worklist scheduling it was built for never landed. Every `add` and
every rebuild pays hash-map maintenance for a structure nothing consumes.

## Design
Delete the field and both maintenance sites. If ISS-381's incremental
rebuild later needs parent pointers, reintroduce them together with their
consumer in the same change — speculative infrastructure without a reader
does not stay.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] `EGraph.uses` and all writes to it are removed.

## Close Notes

Deleted the `uses` field, its initialization, the use-def recording block in
`EGraph::add`, and the reconstruction block in `EGraph::rebuild`. The field
was private, so no public interface changed. Verified with
`moon check --target native` and the full egraph (190/190) and milkir
(173/173) native suites. If ISS-381's incremental rebuild needs parent
pointers, they arrive together with their consumer.
