## Description
Linux native CI for cd0b6ee3 rejects the large sparse root-mask fixture with
IncorrectValue(v646, p0). The same failure reproduces by cross-compiling its
original WAT on AArch64; 255 reference parameters is the first failing boundary.

## Cause and repair
The address is reloaded before a repeated-store instruction, but its split
Late segment and an Early temporary choose the same natural register. The
Early definition overwrites the address's verifier identity before its Late
read. Physical edits occur before/after the complete instruction.

Repair local operand assignments when their phase requirements overlap. Keep
fixed/tied readers in place, select a distinct temporary for the movable
operand, and let AnyLocation readers use an admitted spill home. Preserve the
allocator verifier and existing splitting, root, liveness and quota contracts.
A broader whole-instruction gap restriction was rejected after it broke a
valid existing repeated-use cold-loop allocation; its source was restored.
