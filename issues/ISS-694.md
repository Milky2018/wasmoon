## Description
Major graph traversal revisits77 million previously seen edges out of137 million
candidates on regex2100. Shared per-identity visit epochs occupy4KiB per page,
and interleaved read-only traces overwrite each other's deduplication state.
A public reproduction returns duplicate identities `[4,2,2]` instead of `[4,2]`.

## Design
Give each trace independently owned sparse visited bitmaps and retain its BFS
worklist. Move collector marks into identity-page bitmaps, with one shared set
of mark primitives and union-preserving batch publication. Structural epochs
and page incarnations guard shortcuts; old handles resolve current identities.
Keep young attempt scopes, incomplete-discovery refusal, quotas and rollback.
No new public API, GC policy change or payload-layout change is intended.
