## Description

The final MachV pipeline is materially slower to compile most AArch64 corpus entries even aside from the exceptional `large_cfg.wast` blowup. The formal report records a 2.3748x corpus compile ratio, with many ordinary workloads between roughly 1.3x and 2.6x.

## Design

After ISS-211 removes the superlinear large-CFG defect, use phase-level paired metrics to identify shared fixed costs and remaining workload-specific regressions. Preserve the final semantic MachV, verifier, Target VCode, allocation, and code-object seams.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] The report separates shared frontend, lowering, verification, regalloc, and emission costs.
- [ ] AArch64 meets the 1.05 corpus and 1.10 per-workload compile-time limits with conclusive confidence bounds.
- [ ] AMD64 is evaluated after ISS-215 produces a complete report; any independent target defect is tracked explicitly.
- [ ] Correctness and code-quality gates remain green.
