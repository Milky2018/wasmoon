## Description

The formal AArch64 report records a 1.1737x runtime corpus ratio with a 1.4397 upper 95% bound. `examples/benchmark.wasm` is about 1.9077x slower, and several other workloads exceed or remain inconclusive against the 1.05 per-workload limit.

## Design

Use paired in-module execution measurements and normalized target VCode/disassembly to attribute regressions to code generation rather than process startup or compilation. Address demonstrated hot-path differences without compromising ABI, trap, GC, or metadata correctness.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Runtime regressions are reproduced with in-module work separated from startup and JIT compilation.
- [ ] Material hot-path instruction and allocation differences are identified.
- [ ] The corpus geometric mean is at most 1.03 with an upper confidence bound at or below the threshold.
- [ ] Every runtime workload is at most 1.05 with conclusive evidence.
- [ ] Correctness and compile/code-size gates do not regress.

## Close Notes

Closed as obsolete rather than as a runtime-parity claim. The retired AArch64 backend is not a supported performance baseline, and its paired thresholds no longer block the current implementation.
