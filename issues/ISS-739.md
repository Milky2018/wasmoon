## Description

Native array.new_data allocates a zero-initialized object and populates
its body afterward. Small numeric arrays repeat the checked element
setter even though the segment span and physical layout are known.

## Design

After the existing segment and layout admission, copy the independent
numeric payload during the ordinary typed allocation attempt. Publish
after initializing the header, payload and alignment padding. Keep
the common allocation checks and fallback for legacy layouts,
zero lengths, and verification mode.
