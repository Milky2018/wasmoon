## Description

The native numeric segment constructor misses the ordinary allocation
watermark. A bounded loop can accumulate objects after an equivalent
ordinary array constructor starts collecting. Short data initialization
also repeats destination resolution for each element.

## Design

Reuse the existing precise allocation safepoint after checked segment
admission. The packed allocator requests collection on a prepared-window
miss. Revalidate the segment after the visitor, and keep one physical
allocation attempt. Preserve legacy, quota and injected fault paths.
Only a packed segment collection may leave already-unreachable payload
headers alone during whole-page retirement and immediate compaction.
Keep marking, stable identities, sparse survivors and checked fallbacks.

Reuse the destination already resolved by array.init_data. Copy a checked
compact numeric span with at least two elements. Keep the single-element,
legacy and short verified setters and their original trap ordering.
