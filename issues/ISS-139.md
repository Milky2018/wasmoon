## Description
After memory, call, and vector operations are separated, constants, integer/floating arithmetic, comparisons, conversions, select, copy, and traps still form a large undifferentiated top-level set. Their semantic categories are repeated manually across verification, optimization, printing, and lowering.

## Design
Introduce a `ScalarOp` hierarchy with explicit arithmetic, comparison, conversion, constant, and misc scalar sub-operations. Parameterize repeated signedness and conversion dimensions where the representation stays readable. Migrate all producers and consumers end to end while preserving instruction identity and floating-point bit semantics.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [x] Repository producers canonicalize scalar operations under ScalarOp.
- [x] Repeated arithmetic, comparison, unary, and conversion dimensions use typed sub-operations.
- [x] Constant identity remains type- and bit-pattern-aware.
- [x] Verifier, optimizer, printer, and lowering tests remain green.
