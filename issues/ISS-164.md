## Description
Replace the copied 222-way MilkIR vector lowering match with family-level lowering that maps semantic lane and operation parameters onto MachV's existing parameterized SIMD instructions.

## Design

Preserve special lowering sequences for comparisons, conversions, relaxed SIMD, and target instructions that do not directly represent the source operation. Unsupported normalized combinations must be rejected by verification and must not silently choose a lowering.
