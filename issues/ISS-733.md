## Description

The AArch64 emitter adjusts the stack separately from saving and
restoring its frame record. Each separate adjustment adds an instruction
to an ordinary native frame and to the tail-call epilogue.

## Design

Use pre-indexed STP and post-indexed LDP for the same 16-byte record.
Retain the stack probes, saved-register addresses, trap locations and
unwind operations. Record the allocation and both saved registers at
the paired store. Invalidate artifacts from the previous emitter.

## Notes

- The leaf frame shrinks by eight bytes. Tail-scalar invocation
  medians decrease from 4.418 to 4.213 ms. The paired median
  improvement is 4.52%. The other three controls remain within the
  declared bound.
- The exact-tree suite identified one additional diagnostic snapshot
  with the old separate stack adjustments. Its full byte assertion
  was updated to the paired opcodes.
