## Description
The direct heap expansion allowance does not bound its shared-reservation
fallback. Each eligible allocation still emits a call, stores and a slow/join
CFG diamond. Large straight-line initializers retain excessive live-range work
and memory after the earlier function-root and repeated-word improvements.

## Design
Add a private shared-reservation work allowance, independently of direct heap
and array preparation bounds. Above it, reuse the existing complete collecting
constructor with unchanged semantics. Keep the public environment constructor,
heap layouts, quota/fault controls, GC/barriers and full root publication intact.
Advance codegen24 so persistent caches reject23; keep format12/ABI8 unchanged.
