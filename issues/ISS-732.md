## Description

The original sign and xchacha20 inputs contain the same unsigned
multiply-high polyfill with an additional high cross term. That term
prevented the recognizer from folding the proven carry chain.

## Design

Retain the cross term and reassociate its modulo-2^64 addition around
a multiply-high operation. Both carry contributions must still match.
Leave the existing proof for other multiply shapes in place and update
the artifact revision.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Compare the original xchacha20 and scalarmult inputs with four
  cold matched pairs each, keeping their assertions.
- [x] Verify the original polyfill outputs across boundary values and
  maintain the ordering of effects and traps.
- [ ] Verify the final platform run.

## Notes

- First-invocation medians change from 1.377 to 0.989 seconds for
  xchacha20 and from 78.216 to 55.748 ms for scalarmult. Median paired
  improvements are 28.1% and 28.7%. Eager TurboFan is the faster
  reference for execution on these inputs at 1.271 s and 71.952 ms.
  Compilation medians stay within 1% in this comparison.
- The original scalar helper uses UMULH on AArch64. Its code size
  drops from 104 to 52 bytes on AArch64 and from 367 to 145 on x64.
  The diagnostic 72-assertion fixture retains the original helper
  body and covers both carries, wrapping, and memory effects.
