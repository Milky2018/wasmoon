## Description

The AArch64 final pipeline emits 63,328 bytes across the cutover corpus versus 40,052 bytes for the fixed legacy pipeline, a 1.5811x total ratio. Several individual workloads also exceed the 1.05 limit, including `large_cfg.wast` at 2.5697x, `matmul.wat` at 1.9420x, and `ref.wast` at 4.6667x.

## Design

Attribute growth to concrete instruction-selection, ABI, move, spill/reload, branch-layout, literal, or metadata decisions. Optimize target VCode and emission without reviving the legacy opcode union or weakening semantic and target verifiers.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [x] Per-function and per-category accounting explains the material code-size deltas.
- [x] Total emitted corpus bytes do not exceed the fixed legacy baseline.
- [x] No individual workload exceeds the 1.05 code-size ratio.
- [x] Runtime, compile-time, correctness, relocation, trap, stack-map, and unwind contracts remain valid.

## Notes
- 2026-07-20: AMD64 evidence is not yet available because the fixed legacy runner fails before producing a report; ISS-215 owns that runner defect.
- 2026-07-20: Final AArch64 allocation and basic target-selection fixes reduce total corpus output to 37,516 bytes versus 40,052 legacy (0.936682x). Individual failures remain: `ref.wast` is 24 versus 12 bytes, `matmul.wat` is 744 versus 552 bytes, and `register_pressure.wast` is 1,780 versus 1,640 bytes. Inspection attributes the material `matmul` delta to missing target patterns such as multiply-add, scaled/register-offset addressing, and strength-reduced power-of-two arithmetic rather than to the allocator's former large-CFG blowup.
- 2026-07-20: AArch64 target-owned selection now fuses single-use integer multiply-add, zero-extended register-offset scalar memory access, power-of-two shifts and unsigned remainders, and encodable branch-comparison immediates. The paired 21-sample report at `/private/tmp/machv-cutover-20260720-target-selection/perf-report.json` reduces `matmul.wat` from 744 to 572 bytes versus 552 legacy (1.036232x), while its compile-time ratio is 1.045751x with a 1.070426 upper bound. Total corpus output falls to 37,152 bytes (0.927594x). The remaining code-size failures are `ref.wast` and `register_pressure.wast`, so this issue remains open.
- 2026-07-21: Unused incoming stack parameters are no longer materialized, adjacent AArch64 callee-saved registers use paired loads/stores, and all-register direct internal calls expose their ABI register affinities to allocation. Calls with stack arguments retain the unified emitter shuffle so stack sources are stored before register destinations can overwrite them. The paired report at `/private/tmp/machv-cutover-20260721-code-size-final/perf-report.json` records `ref.wast` at 4 versus 12 bytes, `register_pressure.wast` at 1,676 versus 1,640 bytes (1.021951x), and the whole corpus at 36,200 versus 40,052 bytes (0.903825x), with no per-workload code-size failure.
