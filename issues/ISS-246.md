## Description

Commit `10af58ed` deleted the paired runner, workload corpus, thresholds, and CI command while ISS-212 and ISS-214 still recorded failing or inconclusive measurements. The missing policy decision was whether a deleted backend remained a product acceptance authority.

## Design

Restore the comparison once to obtain complete evidence, then make the authority decision explicitly. The retired backend is not a release or migration oracle; remove its blocking runner and retain actionable current-version performance work separately.
