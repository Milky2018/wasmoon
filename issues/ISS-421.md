# ISS-421: Separate AArch64 scratch roles and frame emergency moves

## Description
Give AArch64 parallel moves a dedicated scratch that cannot occur in allocation
edits, and add an optional raw emergency move area to the target frame.

## Design
Reserve X15 and V18 as the integer and FP/vector move-transfer scratches. Keep
X16 address-only and retain X17/V16/V17 for instruction, call-target, and
regalloc spill uses. Frame planning derives whether any move group requires a
16-byte aligned emergency area and verifies that area does not overlap another
frame region.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Move-transfer scratches are excluded from ordinary allocation and regalloc spill edits.
- [ ] Address and instruction scratch roles remain disjoint from move-transfer roles.
- [ ] Frames without emergency moves keep their previous size.
- [ ] Frames with emergency moves contain one verified 16-byte aligned raw area.
- [ ] Small and large frame offsets are covered by tests.
