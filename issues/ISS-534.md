## Description
Add controlled socket descriptor injection and complete accept, receive, send, shutdown and readiness integration for supported socket kinds.

## Design
Reuse the existing host I/O and directory-capability primitives. Keep P1 rights and error semantics at the adapter boundary. Preserve upstream fixtures and keep Wasmtime-specific hostcall fuel out of scope.
