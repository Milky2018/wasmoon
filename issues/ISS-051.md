## Description
Both `modules/regalloc/unionfind` and `modules/machv_regalloc/unionfind` contain a UnionFind implementation. The duplicate under `machv_regalloc` is a clear ownership leak: UnionFind is a pure algorithm helper and should be owned once by `regalloc`.

## Design
Switch `machv_regalloc` imports from `Milky2018/machv_regalloc/unionfind` to `Milky2018/regalloc/unionfind`, then delete `modules/machv_regalloc/unionfind`. Keep this mechanical: do not change UnionFind semantics or allocator behavior.
