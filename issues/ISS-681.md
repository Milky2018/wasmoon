## Description
The complete corpus screen identifies randombytes at about 1.97x Wasmtime
cold wall time. Two CPU samples point to table-call resolution, canonical type
lookup and Store-to-native lookup, not the initially suspected random_get byte
copy. Callable identities currently pay binary-search costs on every call.

## Design
Build a bounded Store-address-to-record-ordinal index in MoonBit, alongside the
existing sorted views. Admit a dense interval only when its span is at most
twice the input population; otherwise retain binary search. Holes carry an
absent sentinel and duplicates retain their first original record. Queries read
current pointers/type identities through the ordinal, not a cached call target.
Preserve bounds, null, subtype, lifetime, replacement and mutation checks.
